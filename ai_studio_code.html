About Mp Hustle

Mp Hustle is a voice for the resilient. After surviving a transformative battle with depression and the loss of his anchor, he has dedicated his life to stripping away the "Mask" and showing others that darkness is only temporary.

His debut memoir, Sorry Mom, serves as both a tribute to his late mother and a manifesto for youth mental health.

"Depression is a liar that tells you that you’ve reached the last page, but it’s actually just a very long, very difficult chapter."

— From Chapter 4: Rock Bottom
